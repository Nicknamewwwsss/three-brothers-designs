These cabinet sizes are an estimate for presentation and planning, not a construction document. Many sizes aren't given on the sketches, so I've chosen standard catalog sizes with fillers that fit the sketched wall lengths. Nothing was field-measured. Before ordering, fabricating or roughing in anything, check all dimensions, clearances, cutouts, utilities and ventilation against the manufacturers' current installation guides and measured drawings. Stone, exotic slab, backsplash and wall colors are placeholders. The sketches mark them TBD or client selection.

    How the sizes were estimated, and what to verify

    Where the sketches left sizes open

    • Widths follow the sketch wherever it gives one. Odd widths are rounded to the nearest standard 3″ size, and the difference goes into a filler. For example, the sketch's 14″ trash becomes BWB15, and its 22″ drawers become B21-4D plus a filler.
    • Wall 1 uppers: the sketch shows 21½″ / 22½″ / 22½″ / 21½″. I used four 21″ boxes with 2″ fillers at each end so they stay symmetrical over the 88″ run. Heights keep the sketch's 20″ / 26″ above-counter stagger.
    • Wall 2 uppers are 42″ tall from 57″ to 99″, which is 21″ above the counter. They're 19″ deep so the hood face stays flush. Left of the hood is a 12″ open display cabinet with a W4242. Right of it are three doors (21 / 24 / 21), which roughly matches the sketch's door spacing.
    • Hood is a 60″ × 27″ enclosure with its bottom at 72″, which puts it 36″ above the range. The sketch looked closer to 41″. I lowered it to a typical capture height; check Wolf's guide for the PL581912.
    • Pantry tall shelving uses standard 30″ / 36″ widths with fillers to make 106″ wall to wall. The 70″ counter run becomes B36 + B33 drawer bases and a 1″ filler.

    Standard heights used everywhere

    • Toe kick 4½″. Base cabinets 34½″ H × 24″ D, with a 1½″ top for 36″ finished height.
    • Every tall cabinet, upper and stacker tops out on one line at 99″. A 3″ scribe or crown molding closes the gap to a ceiling of about 102″. If the field ceiling differs, only that molding changes.
    • Tall appliance cabinets and tall storage are 84″ H with 15″ flip-up stackers (W3015-24). Pantry tall shelving is 96″ H with a 3″ top filler.
    • Wall 5's wine bay uses a 15″ opening with 3″ fillers each side. Above it is a 21″ × 48″ × 18″ cabinet and a 15″ flip-up.
    • The codes are generic (B, W, SB, BWB, BBC, U, OV, F/TF/WF fillers, REP end panel). In ProKitchen, pick the matching items from the line you're quoting. Depth reductions (19″, 17″, 18″) and SB39 depend on what the line offers.

    Verify before ordering

    • MeasureField-measure every wall, the ceiling height and level, window and door casings, and the dining-wall door before finalizing widths. These sizes are scaled from unscaled hand sketches.
    • ChangedThese sketches differ from the earlier appliance request (one 48″ refrigerator, a Cove dishwasher, 24″ wine). Confirm the client has approved the change.
    • ConfirmRefrigerators: Sub-Zero DET3050CI already has an ice maker in its freezer. Confirm what the "ice maker" drawer on p. 1 means, and confirm the hinge sides (/L, /R). Get the panel-kit and filler requirements from Sub-Zero's install guide.
    • ConfirmAppliance cut-outs for the oven cabinet (MDD3050PE, SO3050PE, WWD30, stacked microwave over oven over warming drawer), the dishwasher openings (Miele 18″ / 24″), the range and the 15″ wine. Use the manufacturers' current specs.
    • ConfirmWine: p. 7 asks for 2 zones. Sub-Zero's 15″ DEU1550W is listed with a single setpoint.
    • ConfirmThe 39″ custom stone sink by MA and its base. Also confirm the blind-corner hardware and that the BBC42-R clears the peninsula, and the pocket-door rough opening.
