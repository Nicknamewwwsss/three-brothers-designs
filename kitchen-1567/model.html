    What changed, assumptions and items to verify

    Rev B changes

    • Cabinets go to the ceiling. Wall cabinets and the oven and refrigerator towers now run to the ceiling. Each has a slab stacker row on top, so there's no gap or soffit above.
    • The hood is flush and built in. It's now a 60" white rift oak slab hood running to the ceiling. Its face is flush with the range-wall cabinets, which are now 19" deep to match. The liner changes to the 19"-deep Wolf PL521912.
    • The existing island is refinished. It keeps its cherry base, marble top and prep sink, all as in the photos. The rope-turned corner posts are removed, leaving plain square corners. The buttons above show four finishes. Charcoal paint is the starting proposal, because it contrasts with the light oak and white marble.
    • New realistic view. It uses the terracotta hex floor, white marble island top and frosted pendants from the photos, with a ceiling, recessed lights and reflections. The concept view is still there.

    Rev A changes from the sketches

    • Two 30" refrigerators (pp. 1, 4) are replaced by one 48" built-in refrigerator at the entrance-hall end of the appliance wall.
    • The microwave / oven / warmer tower is now a 30" speed oven over a 30" single wall oven, matching Wolf M Series.
    • The 18" dishwasher at the butler's station is replaced by the 24" undercounter wine storage. The butler's run is re-balanced: open shelves, wine, sink, trash, drawers.
    • Hood "TBD 60" wide" (p. 2) is now a Wolf 54" pro hood liner inside a 60" white rift oak hood.
    • The one requested dishwasher, a Cove 24", goes at the peninsula, where p. 3 already shows a 24" dishwasher beside the 39" stone sink.
    • The range wall drawers, blind corner, peninsula order and the existing 62×104 island follow the sketches.

    Assumptions in this model

    • The ceiling is assumed to be about 9 ft. Cabinets run to it with a scribe, and wall cabinets start at about 54". The photos show crown molding at the ceiling, which would come off along the cabinet walls.
    • The terracotta hex floor, marble island top, island prep sink and pendants are shown as existing, based on the photos.
    • The peninsula's stool side is drawn as a raised bar-height ledge, per the "bar height stools" note on p. 3.
    • The refrigerator is shown panel-ready with oak slab panels. The toggle above shows the stainless version.
    • The perimeter countertop and backsplash weren't specified. The realistic view shows white marble to match the island top, as a placeholder. The butler's sink fixture is also TBD.
    • The pantry is outside this visualization (marked "TBD" on p. 4).

    Verify before ordering

    • ConfirmWolf does not make refrigerators. Refrigeration in this family is Sub-Zero, so the 48" unit is shown as Sub-Zero CL4850UFD/O. Confirm the style: French door, side-by-side or column pair.
    • ConfirmRange configuration (6 burners + griddle shown) and fuel type. Liquid propane adds -LP.
    • ConfirmThe flush hood uses the 19"-deep liner PL521912. A 22" liner captures more over a 48" range, but it would stick out past 19" cabinets. Check capture against Wolf's ventilation guide, then choose the blower and duct route (p. 4 shows a duct chase in the pantry).
    • ConfirmThe range-wall cabinets are 19" deep so they sit flush with the hood. That puts them about 6" further out over the counter than standard 12–13" cabinets.
    • ConfirmCeiling height and level along every cabinet wall, for the ceiling-height stackers and scribe.
    • ConfirmIsland refinish: the cherry would need stripping or sanding before paint or a lighter stain. Confirm the marble top and prep sink stay in place during the work.
    • ConfirmWine storage hinge side (/L or /R) and whether Cove and wine use oak panels or stainless door kits.
    • ConfirmOven tower electrical, the 48" refrigerator opening and the dishwasher next to the apron sink all need field verification.
